The Month, Day by Day

Every month opens on Feastday, and the year is fifty-six weeks exactly — 392 days, twelve months, one of them long. Choose a day to read what the book files for it.

The Two Horoscopes

The book files one horoscope for every day of the year, under a single heading — The moon and the suns, daily — built, as filed, from the white moon's phase and the suns. It does not file them separately, and this page does not separate them on its own authority. The filed line for the day you are viewing:

The Suns

A horoscope from the number of suns that rose today.

Disputed: the book does not say. No count of suns, and no table of their rising, is filed for any day. The suns of the filed horoscopes are plural and unnamed, and this page invents no number for them.

What the book does file for the suns, it files under Sunsday, the Second Look: “The Temple of the Suns opens early and stays bright late.” Where today's filed line above speaks of the suns, it speaks with that authority and no other.

The Moon

A horoscope from the mood of the moon.

The white moon today:

Disputed: the book does not say. The book names the moon's phases — new on the 4th, first quarter on the 11th, full on the 18th, last quarter on the 25th, always on Moonsdays — but it files no list of named moods. The moon's mood for a day is carried in that day's filed horoscope line, above, and nowhere else.
