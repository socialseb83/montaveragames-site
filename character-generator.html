This window produces Form F-1 · The File Jacket

The city has a file on you. The file is incomplete, which is the best thing a file can be. This window completes it — or as much of it as the city is willing to complete before you have lived here a while.

Everything strange in Montavera was already strange before you arrived. The drawers sing. The dead file complaints. The clocks keep time with holes in it. What the city has always needed is people willing to get involved.

LAW · EVERY PLAYER CHARACTER IS HUMAN.

Not human-passing. Not human with a strange grandparent. Human. The strange is the setting. The human is the story.

LAW · NO CLASSES. NO LEVELS. NO CHOSEN ONES.

You do not select a class that determines what sort of adventurer you are. No prophecy has reserved a place for you. You choose a kind of life. What makes one citizen different from another is three things: their trade, their ear, and their debt — plus their bonds, their pressures, and the part of their life the city keeps sealed.

Every new citizen begins at rank PENDING — zero runes, no file, nobody. The first filing, to rank FILED, happens through play, at the end of the first session. Not here. Here, we prepare the jacket.

Station 1 of 6 · Field One

Your Trade

Choose a trade that interests you, troubles you, or feels uncomfortably familiar. Your trade is the work you did before the city began paying unusual attention to you. Each trade gives you three things: Knowledge — what experience lets you understand; Connections — who recognizes, admits, or employs you; and a Knack — a permission, not a bonus. A knack lets you attempt something with unusual confidence or access. It never guarantees success.

A New Filing

Build the trade in the same structure as the ten above. The Office accepts new filings; your Storyteller will witness it at the table.

Station 2 of 6 · Field Two

The Ear

Everyone in Montavera hears a little. You are someone who did not — or could not — stop hearing. There are exactly two ways it begins. The Bureau recognizes both. It prefers neither.

The Voice You Hear Most Clearly

Choose one of the Seven Voices — the first aspect of the music that made itself unmistakable to you, where your attention naturally goes. This is not a score, not a permanent specialty, and not a promise that you will always hear the truth. Each Voice asks its question of everything it touches.

The Fragment You Were Taught

You begin with one musical fragment that someone taught you. The Office recognizes seven. (A grandmother hummed a Sustain while shelling peas. A Motif may be pressed into your hands like a coin.)

Their name for it, not the Office's. Both will be filed.

LAW · A GIFT HAS A HISTORY.

The teaching is recorded on your file. It does not automatically create a debt. Perhaps it was a promised visit, a kept secret, simple knowledge that the teacher will one day need you to listen. If it creates affection, gratitude, resentment, or uncertainty, the Office records that instead.

What did the gift create?

Station 3 of 6 · Field Three

Your Debt

A useful debt is specific, and has room to change: settleable, but not tomorrow, and not without consequence. A debt that can never be settled is a sentence. Neither is forbidden. Know which one you are choosing.

Station 4 of 6 · Field Four

Your Bonds

Two people who make you a known citizen. Montavera runs on being known: doors open for people whose people answer letters, and merchants extend credit to the bonded. Place them in the city — on a street you can walk to. A bond is not armor. It is a place the game can reach you. Spend carefully. The city keeps the carbon.

Station 5 of 6 · Field Five

Your Pressures

The Open Petition · Form P-1

Every citizen holds one open petition at a time: a single sentence — what you want the city to notice about you. Filed examples: “I want the city to notice that I keep the night market safe.” · “I want the city to notice my rain-songs.” Petitions may be amended. They are never erased. If you filed a Want above, your petition is usually that want, stated for the record.

Six entries. You fill three now. The city keeps the other three sealed, and no window — this one included — will open them for you. They are discovered through play. When a sealed entry is finally filled, you decide what it means. The city provides the fact. The meaning is yours.
