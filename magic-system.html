Sèlanar — the Tongue and the Door

In Montavera, magic is not cast. It is sung, in a tongue where tone is tense: sing a word low and you speak of what is finished, and command it; sing it high and you speak of what is coming, and ask. Every thing has a true name, and a true name is a song. The books walk through that door. This page is how a newcomer learns to knock.

Màranven closes with a primer written for storytellers: the tongue's tones and how they bend meaning, the vowel hand and the hand signs, the doors and how they are asked after — everything a teller needs to put Sèlanar on the table, or on the page, and have it behave.
